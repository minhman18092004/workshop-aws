## Presentations & Main Highlights

### 1. Market Overview and Industry Trends
* **Details:** The speakers discussed the growing "Cloud First" trend. Although the initial cost of cloud projects is significantly lower than traditional on-premise infrastructure, the overall market volume has expanded rapidly, demonstrating that cloud computing has become an irreversible industry trend.

### 2. Data Engineering Perspective
* **Details:** Mr. Bành Cẩm Vĩnh shared his five years of experience as a Data Engineer, from startups to large enterprises such as Zalobay. The key takeaway was the importance of designing scalable and reliable systems while understanding business requirements to maximize the value of data.

### 3. Mindset and Skills for Students
* **Details:** The speakers discussed the common concerns students face when entering the workforce, emphasizing the importance of asking "why" to develop critical thinking and maintaining a "Stay Hungry" mindset—continuously striving to exceed expectations rather than simply completing assigned tasks.
