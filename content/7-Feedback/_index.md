### Overall Evaluation

During my time participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program, I had the opportunity to work in a professional and friendly environment with continuous support from my mentor and team members. The tasks assigned to me were closely aligned with my field of study, allowing me to apply the knowledge I had learned in practice while further developing my research, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Overall, this internship was a valuable learning experience that gave me a better understanding of professional working processes and allowed me to gain practical experience. In the future, I hope the program will organize more in-depth technical sharing sessions, hands-on workshops, and networking activities for interns to enhance learning and collaboration. In general, I believe this is a high-quality program that provides an excellent opportunity for students to develop their professional knowledge and practical skills before entering the workplace.
